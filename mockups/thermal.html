Components for OpenHealth, drawn in the vernacular of a dimensioned drawing. A criterion is a tolerance band, your value is a tick, and the distance between them is annotated the way a drawing annotates a distance. Annotation tracks what you can act on: a met criterion gets none at all, a wide miss gets a wash, and a near miss gets the full dimension — because that is the one worth a conversation.

Verdict marks

Each one pairs a shape, a word and a position on the scale, so none of them needs colour to be read. Confirm it with the greyscale button above.

Patient-side wording. Not read rather than UNKNOWN, because it says what happened instead of naming an enum. Not met rather than FAIL, because the trial failed to match you as much as you failed to match it.

The description

The sentence under that number, drafted five ways and measured against docs/COPY.md — twelve words for this slot, and eight rules taken from how Apple writes interface text: say the one thing the screen is for, never caption what the graphic already draws, and spend no words on feeling. Four of these are drawn in construction weight because they are not shipping.

Shortening is subtraction of jobs, not of words. A carried four: name the marker, size the gap, name the trial, summarise the rest. Three of them were already being done on the card by the number, the scale and the audit — so six words is not a terser A, it is A with its duplicated work deleted. D is the other edge: cut the words without moving the job anywhere and the string stops saying anything. Each verdict names its rule in words, never in colour alone, so the greyscale check at the top of the sheet still reads them.

Audit row

The product. Every row is one of the trial's own eligibility sentences resolved against your number, and every row opens onto the sentence it came from. Not-read rows sort first, because they are the ones you can still do something about; the near miss is the only row that carries a fill.

NCT06942208, inclusion 5, verbatim

"Low-density lipoprotein cholesterol < 100 mg/dL while on stable therapy for at least 4 weeks."

NCT06942208, inclusion 7, verbatim
Structure encodes the content. The only rules are row boundaries and the only indent is the quote bar on a source sentence. Nothing is numbered, because a trial's criteria are a set and not a sequence. An unread value is a hatch, never a dash and never a zero — a dash reads as "none", and "none" is a measurement this app did not take.

Trial row

A tally of criteria rather than a percentage: it reports what the engine actually checked, and leaves no room to read a number as a chance of getting in. Unread criteria stay visible on the row instead of being folded into the count.

Score and confidence

The score is a summary of the scales, so it sits below them and never outgrows the one quantity a screen is about. Confidence keeps its own line and is never folded in.

Controls

One filled button per screen, on the single thing worth doing. Everything else is a hairline ghost or plain text, and a button says exactly what happens and never more.

Radius carries hierarchy instead of being one value everywhere: 16px on a sheet, 10px on a card, 8px on a button, 4px on a tag, and 0 on anything that measures. Sheets lift on a single soft shadow; nothing else in the system has one.
Three tabs, named the way a person would name them. The current one is marked by a red rule and full-ink type, so it still reads with colour removed.

Empty state

An empty screen is an invitation to act. It does not apologise, and it is never vague about what happened.

No documents yet

Add a lab report and the engine checks it against every trial it holds. You never tell it what you have.

No illustration and no centred icon circle. The sentence carries it.

Motion

One orchestrated moment, and it depicts the thing the app does: extension lines drop from the tick and the threshold, the dimension line sweeps open between them, and the margin tag lands. 520ms end to end, staggered 70ms per row. Nothing else in the app moves on its own — no section entrances, no card hovers, no animated ring. Press and expand feedback stays, because it answers something a person did. All of it off under prefers-reduced-motion.
